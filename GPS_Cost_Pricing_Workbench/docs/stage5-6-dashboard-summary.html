GPS Pricing v5.0 · June 2026

After Sales Cost is calculated, the GPS pricing team faces three questions: what price should we set? (competitive yet profitable) · is the price structure sound? (no inversions, no outliers) · how do we track decisions over time? (audit, trends, history).

💲
Stage 5
List Price Derivation
Problem: How to translate internal Sales Cost into a market-competitive List Price while protecting GP% targets?
  • PFV benchmark import — market reference prices by Tier × Term × Geo
  • Dual pricing models: C→L (cost-driven) and L→C (market-driven)
  • Multi-dimensional GP Floor rules — Country × SLA × Term granularity
  • Auto-calculates Channel Price, List Price, GP% per PN
  • One-click bridge to Stage 6 for validation
📊
Stage 6
Gradient Validation
Problem: After deriving prices, are they structurally sound? Higher tiers must cost more. Ratios must make sense. Cross-geo pricing must be consistent.
  • L1 Monotonicity — Tier ↑ → Price ↑, no inversions
  • L2 Ratio Bands — Inter-tier/term ratios within acceptable range
  • L3 Cross-Geo — Same product across countries: deviation < 20%
  • Pass / Warn / Fail per PN with detailed violation explanation
  • Excel export for review and sign-off
📈
Dashboard
History & Audit
Problem: Pricing decisions happen across cycles. Without history, there's no trend analysis, no accountability, no way to learn from past pricing.
  • Cycle-level overview — all PNs, prices, GP% per cycle at a glance
  • PN trend view — GP% change across cycles (▲/▼ Xpp)
  • Field-level audit trail — every value change logged (who, when, old→new)
  • CSV bidirectional sync — export → edit in Excel → import back
  • SQLite persistence — data survives browser refresh, no server needed
What This Means
End-to-End Sales Cost → List Price → Gradient Check, one pipeline
Fully Auditable Every calc, rule, override — logged & traceable
Offline-First Browser-based SQLite. No server, no login.
