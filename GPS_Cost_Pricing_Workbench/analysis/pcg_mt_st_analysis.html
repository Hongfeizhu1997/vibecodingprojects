❶Service Tier determines release timing more than Brand alone — same Brand, different Tier = completely different calendar.
❷TP Mainstream: extreme Mar-Apr concentration — 64.5% of annual releases in just 2 months. Jul–Nov = zero releases.
❸TC DT Entry: summer-only window — 60.7% in Jun+Aug. No Q1, no Q4 releases. Completely opposite to TC DT Mainstream (Mar+Jul peaks).
❹Idea NB tiers: Q4-heavy but staggered — Mainstream peaks Oct (18.8%), Entry peaks Sep (23.2%), Halo peaks Oct-Nov (52%).
❺ThinkSmart: dual-peak Nov+Mar — 34% Nov (holiday) + 29.8% Mar (enterprise refresh). Unique among all tiers.
❻SMB Entry: Jan+Nov+Dec — 16.7% Jan + 15.3% Nov + 16.7% Dec = 48.7% in bookend months. No Oct releases.
❼ThinkPad × 3 Service Tiers = 3 different calendars — Mar-Apr burst (Mainstream), Feb-Apr wave (Entry), Jan-Mar burst (Halo).
❽ThinkCentre × 7 Service Tiers = 3 distinct cluster patterns — Q1 cluster (DT Mainstream, AIO Halo), summer cluster (DT Entry, AIO Entry), spread (DT Halo).
