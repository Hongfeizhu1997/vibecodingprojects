SSG Service Pen Rate Methodology Comparison

Same formula: Service Price = HW Price × Pen Rate  —  Two ways to derive the Pen Rate

ISG Service

Fixed Pen Rate Lookup · Rule-Driven
How It Works

Pricing team manually defines Pen Rate values in a multi-dimensional matrix. Given a dimension combination → look up a fixed percentage.

Pros
  • Fully controlled — business can directly adjust every cell
  • Fully explainable — any quote traces back to a specific rule
  • Fast deployment — no model training or validation cycle
  • Suits low-frequency, high-value deals (ISG: large per-transaction amount)
Cons
  • Dimension explosion makes the table unmaintainable (N dims × M values)
  • Manual updates cannot keep pace with market shifts
  • No notion of "optimal" — only "what the business thinks is reasonable"
  • Cannot leverage historical transaction data to improve pricing

IDG Service

AI-Computed Pen Rate · Model-Driven
How It Works

AI model dynamically outputs a Pen Rate recommendation based on historical transactions, competitive pricing, and customer features.

Pros
  • Automatically captures non-linear relationships across dimensions
  • Learns "what price wins" from historical transaction data
  • Real-time adaptation to market dynamics
  • Suits high-frequency, high-volume scenarios (IDG: many small transactions)
Cons
  • Black-box risk — business may not understand why the model recommends a given price
  • Cold-start problem — meaningless without sufficient historical data
  • Requires continuous monitoring and iteration; high maintenance cost
  • Model drift risk for edge cases (new product categories / atypical customers)

Why ISG Uses Fixed Pen Rate

ISG transactions are low-frequency, high-value (hundreds of thousands to millions USD). Every deal deserves human judgment. Fixed lookup + manual override offers higher fault tolerance — "business-controllable" matters more than "statistically optimal."

Why IDG Wants AI Pen Rate

IDG transactions are high-frequency, low-value (thousands to tens of thousands USD). Humans cannot judge every deal. AI's scale advantage shines here — discovering the price-to-win relationship from massive transaction history.
