IDG vs ISG — Dynamic Pricing Approach Comparison

1. What Dynamic Pricing Aims to Achieve
Service Price = HW Price × Pen Rate
At the B2B quoting stage, the engine automatically outputs a Price Band (Floor / Target / Stretch) for each service PN. The recommended price moves with HW price: when HW price rises, service price follows; when HW is discounted, Pen Rate is adjusted upward to prevent service revenue from shrinking proportionally.
Profitable  Net Price ≥ Cost
Reasonable  Net Price ≤ List Price
Controlled  DOA triggers manual approval
2. Current Approaches: IDG vs ISG

ISG — Fixed Lookup Table

Rule-Driven · Pen Rate defined manually

Pricing team defines Pen Rate values in a multi-dimensional matrix. Given dimension combination → look up a fixed percentage. Suits low-frequency, high-value deals ($50K–$500K+) where every deal deserves human judgment.

IDG — AI Computation

Model-Driven · Pen Rate learned from data

AI model dynamically outputs a Pen Rate recommendation based on historical transactions, competitive pricing, and customer features. Suits high-frequency, low-value deals ($500–$5K) where humans cannot judge every deal.

4. Pros & Cons

ISG — Fixed Lookup

Rule-Driven
Pros
  • Fast deployment — no historical data dependency
  • Fully controllable & auditable — approval-friendly
  • Suits high-value deals — conservative approach is safer
Cons
  • Dimension explosion → unmaintainable at scale
  • No notion of "optimal" — only "what business deems reasonable"
  • Cannot leverage historical transaction data
  • PenRate static when HW price moves

IDG — AI Computation

Model-Driven
Pros
  • Auto-adapts to HW price fluctuations
  • Captures non-linear patterns — different willingness-to-pay across models
  • Continuous learning — every win/loss improves the model
  • Scale advantage — accuracy improves with transaction volume
Cons
  • Cold-start problem — falls back to rules in new markets
  • Requires substantial training data
  • Weak explainability — hard to justify "why 12%"
  • Needs ongoing monitoring & retraining
