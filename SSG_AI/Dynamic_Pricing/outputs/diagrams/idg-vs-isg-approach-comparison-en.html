06 · IDG E-com B2C
Pros & Cons

✅ Pros

Scalable10 Bands govern all SKUs — new products auto-priced, zero manual work
ConsistencySame Band = Same Price — eliminates human judgment variance
ExplainableEvery factor traceable — "Why $280? Because Band E × 3Y × PS"
Self-EvolvingQuarterly calibration + Attach Rate loop → auto-adapts to market
Low OpsPost-launch manual maintenance ≈ 0
Risk Ctrlα/γ prevents long-term loss; Floor/Cap prevents price drift
AcademicLogarithmic curve backed by behavioral science

❌ Cons

Cold StartHeavy upfront work: Band boundaries, anchors, α/γ calibration
Band RigidityHW near boundary may jump price($1299 vs $1301)
No NegotiationAlgorithmic price has no flexibility — "system says this price"
Data SensitiveBad HW AUR → entire pricing table distorted
Domain Knowledgeα/γ needs Delivery team cost curve input
No BundlingCannot do "buy 3Y PS, get 1Y ADP free"
07 · ISG B2B
Pros & Cons

✅ Pros

Ultra-SimpleOne formula, one table — understood in 5 minutes
FlexibleKey account pressure, competitor moves — instant human PenRate adjustment
Negotiation-Friendly"I got you a special PenRate" has a human touch
Low BarrierNo modeling/training needed — experienced pricing manager + Excel
Complex DealsBundling, discounting, cross-PN packaging — humans fold it all in
Fast StartHistorical PenRate data is all you need

❌ Cons

Not ScalableEach new Dimensioncombo → human judgment → linear cost growth
InconsistentTwo managers may set different PenRates for the same scenario
Knowledge Loss"Why 18%?" — answer lives in the manager's head
Black BoxPenRate is just a number — no auditable derivation trail
Slow ResponseHW price change → wait for human → update table → take effect
No Self-OptimizationCannot learn from won/lost deal data automatically
Human RiskManager misses margin floor → wrong PenRate
08 · Architectural Philosophy
Two Design Philosophies
IDG · Algorithm-Driven
"Encode knowledge into the system"
Let algorithms make repetitive decisions
Auto-calibrate to adapt to change
Consistency > Flexibility
Invest early, save later
vs
ISG · PenRate-Driven
"Keep knowledge with the experts"
Let humans make holistic judgments
Manual assessment to respond to change
Flexibility > Consistency
Save early, pay later
09 · Conclusion & Cross-Learning
Scenario Fit, Not Better or Worse
IDG: B2C E-com → Standardized × High Volume × Zero Human Touch → Algorithm-driven is optimal
ISG: B2B Deals → Customized × Low Volume × High-Touch Negotiation → PenRate is the pragmatic choice
ISG Can Take One Step Forward
Decompose PenRate from a black-box number into multiple explicit Dimension
Band × Segment × Term, data-driven dimension by dimension
Keep B2B flexibility while gaining algorithmic Scalability
