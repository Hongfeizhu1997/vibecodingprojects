Service Price = HW Unit Price × Pen Rate
Service price moves with hardware price. When HW price rises, service follows; when HW price drops, Pen Rate adjusts upward to protect service revenue from proportional erosion. IDG uses the same tiered lookup methodology as ISG, with 3 additional service-specific delegation dimensions.

Phase 1: Tiered — Zero-Risk Quick Win
Deploy immediately — no historical data needed. Define 3–5 HW price bands with fixed Pen Rate per band. Sales team can reference a one-page table. ISG already uses this method — IDG reuses the same approach with 3 extra service dimensions.

Cliff mitigation: set band boundaries at natural product price gaps (e.g. $15K between mainstream and workstation lines) to minimize edge cases.
Phase 2: Power Law — AI-Driven Upgrade
Single parameter k controls everything. k=0.3 → mild adjustment, k=0.5 → balanced, k=0.7 → aggressive service revenue protection. Different Service Tiers can run different k values — premium support gets higher k (more protection).

Eliminates cliff effect entirely while preserving business intuition. Calibrate k from Phase 1 transaction data via log-log regression.
Key Risk: Over-Smoothing vs. Business Control
Smooth curves (Power Law, Exponential) look elegant but harder to audit. Business stakeholders may prefer visible, explainable tiers over mathematically optimal curves. Phase 1 builds trust with transparent rules; Phase 2 introduces optimization only after stakeholders are comfortable.

Recommendation: run both in parallel during transition — Tiered as floor, Power Law as ceiling.
