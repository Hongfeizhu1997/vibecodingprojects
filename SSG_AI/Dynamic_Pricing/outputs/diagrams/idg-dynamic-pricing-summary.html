IDG Dynamic Pricing — Solution Overview

B2B · LBPi · LBPe | Phased: Rule-Based Q.Win → AI-Driven
Service Price = HW Unit Price × Pen Rate
Service price moves with hardware price. When HW price rises, service follows; when HW price drops, Pen Rate adjusts upward to protect service revenue from proportional erosion. IDG uses the same tiered lookup methodology as ISG, with 3 additional service-specific delegation dimensions.
Pen Rate Management in LBP (3 Core Components)
01
Pen Rate Delegation
  • 6-dimension rule matrix: Service OH · Service Tier · Service Sub Tier · BU · Country · GTM
  • Service dimensions (OH / Tier / Sub Tier) capture cost & complexity variance across hundreds of PC SKUs
  • Bulk import/export with full audit history
02
Pen Rate Adjustment
  • Tiered adjustment of Pen Rate% by HW unit price band
  • Same standard Pen Rate, higher effective rate for higher-value HW
  • More expensive hardware justifies higher service value
⭐
Priority Logic
  • Auto-resolves conflicts when multiple rules match across all 6 dimensions
  • Exact-match priority: Service Tier → Service Sub Tier → Service OH → BU → Country → GTM
  • Higher-granularity rules (Tier-level) override broader rules (BU-level)
