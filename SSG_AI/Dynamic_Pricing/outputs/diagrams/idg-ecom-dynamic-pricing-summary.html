IDG E-com Dynamic Pricing — Solution Overview

B2C · SMB & Consumer · WW | v2.0 — Value Band + Term Curve Model
Service Price = BasePrice(HW_Band, Tier) × TermFactor(Term) × αγ(Term, Tier)
Service price scales with hardware value and term length. HW is bucketed into AUR bands — more expensive devices get proportionally higher service prices. A logarithmic term curve reflects diminishing willingness to pay for extra years, while α and γ cost adjustments protect margin on longer PSP contracts.
4 Core Components
01
HW Value Banding
Groups hardware by actual AUR into 8–10 bands (A–J). Each band gets a fixed Base 3Y PS/PSP anchor price — ~15% of mid-AUR. SMB and Consumer have separate band tables.
02
Term Factor Curve
Logarithmic willingness-to-pay model: TermFactor = 0.55 × ln(Term) + 0.65. 3Y = 1.0 baseline; 1Y ≈ 0.52×; 5Y ≈ 1.23×. Matches real customer attach behaviour — early years valued most.
⭐
Cost Adjustments (α, γ)
α: steady cost rise after year 3 (labour, parts inflation). γ: tail-risk bump after year 4 (EOL parts, sealed battery). Only applied to Onsite/PS/PSP. Depot stays flat. Bridges customer psychology with real cost.
04
Auto-Calibration
Quarterly refresh: pull latest HW AUR distribution → re-bucket → update 3Y anchors → regenerate all SKUs. Optional AI tuning adjusts band coefficients based on attach rate movements.

α & γ Parameters — by Service Tier
Depot — α=0, γ=0
No age sensitivity. Mail-in repair costs don't rise with device age. Term curve stays purely behavioural.
Onsite — α=0.02, γ=0
Mild labour inflation only. Onsite dispatch costs rise gently after year 3, no tail-risk spike.
Premier Support — α=0.03–0.04, γ=0.03–0.05
Predictable support cost escalation. Both steady rise and moderate tail risk from parts scarcity on older devices.
Premier Support Plus — α=0.07–0.09, γ=0.08–0.10
ADP + sealed battery coverage creates real cost spikes beyond year 3. Highest αγ values protect margin against heavy claims on aging hardware.
💡
Service price tracks both device value and duration value. The model fixes the core problem: today HW AUR fluctuates ±25% within a tier, but service prices stay flat — yielding a near-zero correlation (r ≈ 0.08) between what customers pay for hardware and what they pay for protection.

Projected impact: +12–15% service revenue (+$10.3M SMB, +$3.2M Consumer across last 4 quarters), with 5+ pts attach improvement on entry SKUs through fairer pricing. Zero risk — stays within existing commercial guardrails.
