04 · Design Principles

Two Principles

Principle 1
Learn, Don't Prescribe
Pen Rates are learned from historical transaction data — not looked up from a static rule table. The engine automatically discovers the optimal Price Band for "X1 Carbon × 3yr Premier × EMEA × Enterprise" without anyone maintaining a manual rulebook.
Principle 2
Reference, Don't Depend
PAIDS HW pricing flows into the engine as one HW Context feature — alongside HW Config, Cost, and PFV. If PAIDS changes or is replaced, only that single input mapping is updated; the 6-step pipeline, Price Band logic, and Guardrail layer remain untouched. The engine reads HW price — it doesn't depend on how that price was made.

07 · Key Scenarios

What This Looks Like in Practice

HW Discount — Margin Intact

EMEA ThinkPad deal with 15% HW discount. Engine raises Pen Rate 5–8pp; Floor holds Margin Floor. Service revenue survives HW discounting.

HW Price Up — Attach Window

HW rises 8% on shortage. Moderated Pen Rate; Target service price up only 5%, lowering TCO perception, boosting attach.

New GEO — No Guesswork

AP with sparse data. Benchmark borrows EMEA priors. Accuracy beats flat rate; Confidence = Low flags caution to sales.
