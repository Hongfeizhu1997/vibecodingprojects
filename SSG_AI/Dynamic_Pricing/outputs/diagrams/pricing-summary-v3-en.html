04 Design Principles

Principle 1
Learn, Don't Prescribe
Pen Rates are learned from historical transaction data — not looked up from a static rule table. The engine automatically discovers the optimal Price Band for "X1 Carbon × 3yr Premier × EMEA × Enterprise" without anyone maintaining a manual rulebook.
Principle 2
Decoupled from HW Pricing
The Dynamic Pricing engine is not coupled to PAIDS (HW pricing system). PAIDS output is just one input to HW Context — the engine independently decides the service Price Band, unbound by HW pricing strategy.

07 Key Scenarios

HW Discounted — Service Margin Intact

EMEA Enterprise ThinkPad deal with 15% HW discount. Engine raises Pen Rate 5–8pp; Floor holds Margin Floor. Service revenue isn't dragged down by HW discounting.

HW Price Up — Attach Window Opens

HW price rises 8% on supply shortage. Engine outputs a moderated Pen Rate; Target service price rises only 5%, lowering TCO perception and boosting attach rate.

New GEO — No Guesswork

AP market has sparse data. Comparable Benchmark shrinks to EMEA priors (price range + win-loss). Accuracy beats global flat rate; Confidence = Low flags caution.
