Scope boundary: v2.0 covers IDG POS (Point of Sale) only — where the customer selects hardware and service together at purchase time. The model prices any standard warranty/upgrade service PN attached to a hardware device, regardless of bundling type or RTM channel. The core pricing engine (Band lookup → Base 3Y → Term Curve → αγ) is bundling-agnostic and RTM-agnostic.

Recurring (2.1.2) is partial: the Term Curve is calibrated for 1–5Y one-off purchases. Recurring subscription models may extend beyond 5 years and require curve extension, though the Band anchor values remain reusable.

Not in scope (outside POS): APOS (After Point of Sale — support.lenovo.com, Vantage), CBSO (Contract-Based recurring services), and all ISG / Hybrid Cloud / DWS / Sustainability / AI / LPS scenarios.
