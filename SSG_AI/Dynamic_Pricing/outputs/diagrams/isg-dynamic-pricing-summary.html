ISG Dynamic Pricing — Solution Overview

B2B · LBPi · WW | Phase 2 Delivered
Service Price = HW Unit Price × Pen Rate
Service price moves with hardware price. When HW price rises, service follows; when HW price drops, Pen Rate adjusts upward to protect service revenue from proportional erosion.
Pen Rate Management in LBP (4 Core Components)
01
Pen Rate Delegation
Manages Pen Rate rules: which BU / Country / GTM gets which Pen Rate. Supports bulk import/export and full audit history.
02
Pen Rate Adjustment
Tiered adjustment of Pen Rate% by HW unit price band. Same standard Pen Rate, higher effective rate for higher-value HW — more expensive hardware justifies higher service value.
⭐
Priority Logic
When multiple rules conflict, auto-resolves by priority. 5 exact-match dimensions: Brand · SDF · Base Warranty · RAD · GTM.
04
Downstream Sync
Pen Rate results automatically synced to LUDP and Magellan, ensuring pricing consistency across quoting and downstream systems.
