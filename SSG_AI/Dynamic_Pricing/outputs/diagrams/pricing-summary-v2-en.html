01 One-Line Positioning

Dynamic Pricing: when HW prices move, service prices move with them — up with HW increases, and when HW is discounted, Pen Rate automatically adjusts so service revenue isn't proportionally eroded.

Four Engine Inputs

  • HW Value Band: $0-500 / $500-1K / $1K-2K / $2K+
  • Service Tier × Term: Service level + contract duration
  • GEO × Segment: Region + customer tier
  • Competitive Signals (Phase 2+): Win-price range by segment

Engine learns optimal Pen Rate from historical transactions, with three guardrails: above cost, below list price, flag outliers for review

07 Key Business Scenarios

Large HW Deal — Margin Intact

EMEA ThinkPad enterprise deal with 15% HW discount. Engine auto-adjusts Pen Rate +5-8pp, defending service revenue floor.

HW Price Hike — Attach Opportunity

HW supply-constrained, price up 8%. Pen Rate eases, service price rise is moderated, lowering total cost perception and boosting attach.

New GEO Launch — No Guesswork

AP new market with sparse data. Model borrows similar GEO (EMEA) experience — accuracy beats global one-size-fits-all.
