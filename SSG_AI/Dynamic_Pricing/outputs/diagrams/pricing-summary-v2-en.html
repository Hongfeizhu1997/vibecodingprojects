03 · Design Principles

Three Principles

Learn, Don't Hard-Code

Pen Rate isn't looked up from a static table — it's learned from historical transactions. The engine discovers: "For X1 Carbon at $2,000 with 3yr Premier Support, the optimal Pen Rate is 10%, not 8%."

Independent From HW Pricing

The Dynamic Pricing engine does not couple with PAIDS. HW price is just one input feature — the engine independently decides service price, not bound by HW pricing strategy.

Progressive Trust

POC: offline validation → Pilot: recommendation as reference → Rollout: auto-fill with manual override → Wave 2: high-confidence auto-approve. Trust builds step by step.

07 · Key Business Scenarios

What This Looks Like in Practice

Large HW Deal — Margin Intact

EMEA ThinkPad enterprise deal with 15% HW discount. Engine auto-adjusts Pen Rate +5-8pp, defending service revenue floor.

HW Price Hike — Attach Opportunity

HW supply-constrained, price up 8%. Pen Rate eases, service price rise is moderated, lowering total cost perception and boosting attach.

New GEO Launch — No Guesswork

AP new market with sparse data. Model borrows similar GEO (EMEA) experience — accuracy beats global one-size-fits-all.
