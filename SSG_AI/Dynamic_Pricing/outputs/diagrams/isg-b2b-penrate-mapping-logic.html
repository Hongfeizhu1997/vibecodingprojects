ISG B2B Pen Rate Mapping — Data Logic & Calculation

Three-table architecture · Role-based delegation · HW-price-driven adjustment
1. Overall Architecture
Table 1
Brand Mapping
Maps a service PN's ProductCategory Level3 → Brand Code (e.g. CSP-TS68, CSP-OEM).
11 rows maintained by MDM.
Table 2
Pen Rate Delegation
Role + 9 business dimensions → Standard Pen Rate% (the ceiling).
Different roles see different rates: LBP-E auto 8–30%, Sales Rep 80–85%.
Table 3
Pen Rate Adjustment
HW Entitled Price band → Adjustment Factor (10–70%).
Higher HW price → higher adjustment → higher effective Pen Rate.

💡
Standard Pen Rate is the ceiling — Adjustment Factor brings it down to reality.
LBP-E auto delegates at 8–30% (conservative baseline). Sales Rep gets 80–85% (negotiation room).
The Adjustment table then scales by HW price: lower HW → lower adjustment, higher HW → higher adjustment.
Final effective Pen Rate typically lands at ~5% for system-generated mid-range quotes — exactly the user-observed figure.

Floor Rule safeguard: Final Delegation = min(Original LBP Delegation, New Pen Rate Price). The new mechanism never inflates quotes.
6. Priority Logic — Multi-Rule Conflict Resolution
BU > Country > EU ID List > GTM Sub-Seg > RAD > Brand > SDF > Warranty
When multiple Delegation rows match the same bid (due to NULL/wildcard dimensions), the row with the highest-priority non-NULL field wins.
