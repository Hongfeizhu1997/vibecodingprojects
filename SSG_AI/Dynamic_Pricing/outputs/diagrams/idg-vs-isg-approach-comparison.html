05 · Design Philosophy
Two Worldviews
IDG · Algorithm-Driven
"Encode knowledge into the system"
Algorithms make repetitive decisions
Auto-calibrate to change
Consistency > Flexibility
Invest early, save later
vs
ISG · PenRate-Driven
"Keep knowledge with the experts"
Humans make holistic judgments
Manual assessment to change
Flexibility > Consistency
Save early, pay later
06 · Conclusion
Scenario Fit, Not Better or Worse

IDG: B2C E-com → Standardized × High Volume × Zero Touch → Algorithm-driven is optimal

ISG: B2B Deals → Customized × Low Volume × High-Touch → PenRate is the pragmatic choice

ISG Can Take One Step Forward

Decompose PenRate from a black-box number into Band × Segment × Term —
keep B2B flexibility while gaining algorithmic scalability.
