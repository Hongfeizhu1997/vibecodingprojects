Next steps

Lifted from 5.5% to 7.4%, a near-perfect match to the 7.0% real share. The under-recommendation of PSP is resolved at this offset.

② 60-month term UNDER-SERVED

Bottom line: PSP +0.35 alone delivers the best exact match (71.7%) and a near-perfect PSP fix — the highest-accuracy configuration. Its one gap is 60-month under-serving. The correct fix is a type-aware 60-month boost (lift 60M for Premier only, shield PSP Server), not a blanket 60-month weight — which would drag PSP back up and push accuracy down.
