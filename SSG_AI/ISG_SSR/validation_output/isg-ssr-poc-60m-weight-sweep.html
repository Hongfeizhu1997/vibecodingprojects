Verdict: the 60M term weight is a blunt instrument. +0.05 closes the 60M gap at a modest cost (exact match 71.7% → 70.7%), but +0.10 overshoots both dimensions — 60M to 53.9% and PSP to 14.8% — while degrading exact match to 68.5%. If more 60M coverage is needed, 0.05 is the ceiling; a type-aware term boost (60M lift applied to Premier only, not PSP Server) would avoid the PSP flip entirely.
