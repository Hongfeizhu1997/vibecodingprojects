I. Error Direction: Is the Model Aggressive or Conservative?

The model is slightly conservative, but the dominant error is term-duration confusion — not up-tier or down-tier bias. Of the 340 missed contracts, 46.8% recommend the wrong term duration (a different product within the same warranty type, e.g. 60M vs 36M), and 31.8% confuse adjacent products that differ only in SLA tier (same type+term, e.g. 24x7 vs NBD). Only 21.5% are genuine cross-type misclassification, with downgrades outnumbering upgrades 1.7:1.
