Part 3: 3 Tuning Decisions — All Push Toward "More Aggressive"

The model is net-conservative: when it's wrong, it recommends cheaper/shorter products 36.2% of the time vs. more expensive/longer 28.2%. The seller can always downgrade. Three tuning decisions correct this bias.
