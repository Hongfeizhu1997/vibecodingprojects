5 · What This Means — Where the Tuning Lands

Next steps

The distribution view maps each gap back to the two tuning knobs. The 60-month weight is a blunt instrument: +0.05 is the ceiling, and the real fix is type-aware.

① 60-month term OVERSHOOT

AI runs 45.4% vs 41.9% real (+3.5pp). At this weight the gap is nearly closed from the under-serving baseline, but any higher weight overshoots further.

② PSP Server COLLATERAL

Lifted to 8.9% vs 7.0% real — not because of the PSP offset (already matched at 7.4%), but because the 60-month weight drags in 60M PSP Server.

③ SLA tier MINOR

Four Hours drifts to 56.4% vs 55.6% real — a mild lockstep shift, because 60-month products skew toward Four Hours.

Bottom line: the PSP +0.35 · 60M +0.05 combination lands at 70.7% exact match, and the distribution stays close to reality — but the 60-month weight overshoots on both 60M (+3.5pp) and PSP Server (+1.9pp). The real fix is a type-aware term boost: apply the 60-month lift to Premier only, and shield PSP Server, so the PSP term flip disappears without paying the accuracy cost.
