06 · SLA Analysis
SLA confusion is symmetric (51:57) — the model cannot distinguish 24×7 from NBD well enough

Finding: Nearly balanced (51:57). The model is not biased toward either SLA tier — it simply cannot tell them apart reliably.
Recommendation: Do not apply directional tuning. Instead, add stronger features: hardware criticality scoring and customer industry vertical to help the model distinguish SLA needs.
Priority: P2 — address after P0 adjustments are validated.
