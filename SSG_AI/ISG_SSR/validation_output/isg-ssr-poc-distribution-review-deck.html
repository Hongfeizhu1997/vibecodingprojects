Reading it for the business: the model knows which product family the customer needs — 78.5% of mismatches stay inside the same type. The remaining gap is about telling apart near-identical products (36M vs 60M, Four Hours vs NBD), exactly what the next round of tuning addresses.
06 · What This Means — Where the Tuning Lands
The distribution view maps the three adjustments directly onto the visible gaps

① Term Duration

P0
The #1 mismatch (46.8%). The 60M→36M direction loses coverage the customer already bought. Fix: default to the longer term when uncertain — seller keeps override.

② PSP Server

P0
Visible in the type comparison: AI under-recommends PSP (−1.6pp). Driven by only 7.1% training share. Fix: augment PSP data + modest PSP preference.

③ SLA Tier

P2
Four Hours ↔ NBD confusion is symmetric (51:57) — no directional bias. Fix: add hardware criticality + industry features, not a tuning tilt.
Bottom line: 85.2% of recommendations are an exact product match, and the overall distribution is near-identical to what customers actually buy. The 14.8% gap is concentrated in two fixable, adjacent-product confusions — term length and SLA tier — that the P0/P2 adjustments target directly.
