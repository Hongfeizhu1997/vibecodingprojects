How Price Influences Recommendations
Standard ML Approach
Recommend most likely service
Low-price, high-frequency services
dominate the results
→ Service revenue at risk
→
ISG SSR Approach
Recommend most likely service
When probabilities are close,
tilt toward higher-priced option
→ Protects service attach revenue
Custom Loss = Accuracy Loss  +  β · Price Tilt
Price Tilt: penalizes low-price services when probabilities are close
β (business dial): 0.5 → 0.1  dynamic adaptive decay  ·  Tunable by business owners

Three Safety Nets
🔀

GBB Branching

Low model confidence (≤0.75) triggers conservative mode — price-sorted options only, no aggressive recommendations.

🛡️

Business Rule Filters

Geo restrictions, EOL detection, compliance rules applied after ML scoring — human-defined, always enforced.

🚦

Deployment Gate

New model must strictly outperform current on Accuracy + F1-Score. Fail → auto-rollback. Consecutive failures → pause.
