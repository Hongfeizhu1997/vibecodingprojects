### 2. Hardware Volume & Service $/HW

### 3. Penetration Rate

  - FY25/26: Q1 6.2%, Q2 6.6%, Q3 6.1%

### 5. Project Launch Cutoff Validation

### 6. Incremental Contribution (Difference-in-Differences)
- For pilot countries, compare average revenue per HW unit in the 4 quarters 
  before launch vs. the quarters after launch
- For non-pilot EMEA, do the same as a control group
- Compute the DiD estimate: (pilot post - pilot pre) minus (non-pilot post - non-pilot pre)
- Compare your DiD result against the stated $4.1M–$6.7M Q3 contribution range

### 7. $/HW Organic Baseline Check

- Project what FY25/26 Q3 $/HW would have been without the project
- Compute: actual $45 minus organic projection, multiply by Q3 HW volume

### 8. Average Contract Value
- Compute average contract value = total service revenue divided by number of 
  service-attached units, per quarter
- Verify the stated series peaks at $738 in Q3 FY25/26
- Confirm Q2 FY25/26 was $652 (lower despite higher penetration rate)
