Interpretation

EMEA leads in both operational and financial metrics — the 6-week pilot phase created a calibrated model that drives both higher seller acceptance and higher service value.

NA has high operational KPIs (53% acceptance) but shows Q4 financial stress ($/HW declined from $51.90 to $51.07). This is the cold-start effect: the model works at the configuration level but hasn't yet translated to revenue because sellers in NA haven't built trust.

AP & LA show lower but consistent operational-to-financial mapping. Their lower $/HW reflects different market structures (lower ASP, different service mix).
