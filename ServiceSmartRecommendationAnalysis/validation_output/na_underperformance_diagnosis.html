The Real Story

NA is the most valuable per-unit GEO in the entire SSR deployment. It has the highest service value per hardware unit, the highest average contract value, and the most premium hardware mix. The "-1.6% YoY" headline is misleading because it compares against a Q4 FY24 baseline ($51.90) that itself was the product of a +42.6% organic growth run during FY24.

The project likely defended $3-6M of NA service revenue in Q4 that would have been lost to W10 EOS normalization. Without the project, NA's $/HW might have dropped to $46-48 — a reversion toward the FY24 Q1-Q3 mean ($36-41) adjusted for the higher HW volume base. The fact that it only dropped to $51.07 suggests the project was stabilizing service attach against a powerful organic headwind.
