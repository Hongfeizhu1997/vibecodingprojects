ASP-to-$/HW Pass-Through Calibration

The handover assumed a fixed 75% pass-through rate from HW ASP changes to service prices. Actual data shows the relationship is highly variable — from -3.9x to +10x. The SSR project itself breaks this relationship by driving $/HW growth independent of hardware prices.

Key insight: In quarters where $/HW change far exceeds ASP change (ratio > 1.5x), the excess is project-driven. EMEA Q3 (5.46x) and NA Q3 (10.01x — organic, W10 EOS) are the clearest signals. The 75% fixed assumption in the handover is not supported by the data.
