Key Cross-GEO Patterns

1. NA is the product trust leader. Same product (3Y Premier Support) gets 52.3% in NA vs 42.5% in EMEA vs 41.9% in AP. 10pp gap on identical product implies seller behavior or market maturity differences, not model quality.

2. Premium products reveal cultural gaps. EMEA sellers trust AI on Premier Support Plus at 65.4% — LA sellers only 34.7%. Same product, same AI engine, 30pp difference. Suggests different regional seller training or incentive structures.

3. AP 4Y Premier Support is a disaster (16.1%). 5,467 configs but sellers reject AI 84% of the time. The 49.6% change rate means sellers actively choose different products — likely downgrading to 3Y or switching type.

4. Longer terms consistently outperform. In every GEO, 3Y < 4Y < 5Y in acceptance. Sellers trust AI more for bigger, longer-term decisions. This is consistent and intuitive.
