Key Insights

▶ Global ESS bookings reached $217.0M in Q4 FY25/26, ▲ +9.3% vs Q3 and ▲ +29.1% vs Q4 FY24/25. This is the highest quarterly ESS booking total to date.
▶ EMEA continues to lead globally and posted its strongest quarter at $98.7M (▲ +8.5% QoQ), driven by both volume growth and rising $/CA.
▶ NA showed the strongest QoQ growth among all GEOs (+19.3%), while LA saw the weakest (-20.3%).
▶ GPS Penetration Rate (ESS bookings as % of HW revenue) was 5.22% globally in Q4 (▼ -1.4% YoY vs Q4 FY24/25). A declining PR% despite growing bookings suggests HW revenue is growing faster than service attachment.
▶ Top offering by ESS bookings in Q4: Support Services at $208.7M. Fastest-growing L2 offering QoQ: Software & Cloud Services (+1230.1%).
