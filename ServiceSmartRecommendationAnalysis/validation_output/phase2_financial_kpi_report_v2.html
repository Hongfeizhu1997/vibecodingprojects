V2 Framework: Constant Service Price Assumption

Core Logic

Assumption: Service product prices are held constant year-over-year.
Implication: Under constant prices, any increase in Service Value per Hardware Unit ($/HW) must come from the project — either through higher service attach rates (more units getting service) or better service tier mix (higher-value recommendations being accepted).
Formula: Project Contribution = $/HW Growth Rate × Service Revenue

Why This Framework?

AdvantageExplanation
SimplicitySingle metric ($/HW growth) replaces complex DiD with subjective attribution rates. Easy to explain to stakeholders.
Direct causal link$/HW growth directly reflects what the project does: recommend better services → higher service value per hardware unit.
Conservative on pricingBy assuming zero service price inflation, all growth is attributed to volume/mix effects — the project's domain.
Consistent across GEOsSame methodology applied uniformly. No need for per-GEO attribution rate calibrations.

Caveats & Assumptions

#AssumptionImplicationMitigation
1Service prices are constant YoYIf service prices increased, $/HW growth overstates project contribution. If prices decreased, it understates.Validate with actual service SKU price lists. If price changes are ≤3%, the framework remains robust.
2All $/HW growth above 0% = projectOrganic $/HW growth exists (NA Q3: +34.7% with zero project). The framework is an upper bound.Compare with control GEOs (quarters without project exposure) to calibrate organic drift.
3Negative $/HW growth = zero contributionNA Q4 shows -1.6% $/HW. The project may have PREVENTED a larger decline — but this defense value is not captured.A penetration defense analysis (as proposed in the handover) could complement this for negative-growth GEOs.
4Hardware mix effects are second-orderIf hardware product mix shifted toward premium devices, $/HW could rise organically (premium devices attract premium services).Check HW ASP change vs $/HW change. When ASP is flat but $/HW is up (e.g. EMEA Q3: ASP +2.3%, $/HW +12.8%), the framework is strongest.
